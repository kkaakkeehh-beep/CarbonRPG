CarbonRPG — Hero of Carbon: an organic chemistry RPG

An organic chemistry quiz RPG for people who know their chemistry, playable in your browser. Your hero is Carbo, an sp³ carbon with four hands. Bond four friends to those hands and the CIP priorities of the substituents decide whether Carbo is (R) or (S). Then take on the Meso Order, a dark organization that wants to erase "handedness" from the world, with organic chemistry questions.

In battle, a right answer damages the enemy and a wrong answer damages you. Every answer comes with an explanation.

What the questions cover (5 chapters, 443 questions)

  • Chapter 1: Nucleophile Forest: SN1, SN2, E1 and E2, stereochemistry, solvent effects, nucleophilicity and leaving groups, carbocation rearrangements, neighboring-group participation
  • Chapter 2: Port Carbonyl: nucleophilic addition, enolates, aldol reactions, Grignard reactions, Wittig reactions, Claisen condensation, Michael addition, Robinson annulation, Baeyer–Villiger oxidation, reductions
  • Chapter 3: The Aromatic Kingdom: aromaticity (Hückel's rule), electrophilic aromatic substitution and directing effects, Friedel–Crafts reactions, SNAr, benzyne, diazonium salts, Birch reduction, radical reactions (NBS, tin hydrides, radical cyclization)
  • Chapter 4: Mirror Cloister: Diels–Alder reactions, electrocyclic reactions, sigmatropic rearrangements, 1,3-dipolar cycloadditions, frontier molecular orbitals (FMO), point groups and symmetry elements, meso compounds, optical resolution
  • Chapter 5: Waste Liquor Town: retrosynthesis and synthesis planning, protecting groups, chemoselectivity, asymmetric synthesis and asymmetric catalysis, chiral auxiliaries, kinetic resolution, ee calculations

Every compound named in a question is drawn as a structure. Some questions are answered by picking a structure (45 questions).

Difficulty

Four levels: Undergrad I, Undergrad II, Grad Entrance Exam and PhD (Brutal). Use it to review university organic chemistry or to prepare for graduate entrance exams. Questions you miss go into the Review Notebook, where you can solve them again with explanations.

FAQ

Is it free?
Yes. No sign-up needed.
Can I play on my phone?
Yes, on a phone or a PC, in your browser. Your progress saves automatically in the browser.
Which languages are available?
Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese and Korean. Switch at the top right of the title screen.
